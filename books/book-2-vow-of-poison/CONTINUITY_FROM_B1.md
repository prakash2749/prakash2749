## 7. Series rules Book 2 must not break

1. ***Verity* is never named in Book 2.** It was spoken once in Book 1. The rotating names and "the same buyer" are as close as Book 2 comes (`SEEDING_MATRIX.md` §8, `WORLD_BIBLE.md` §8).
2. **Nobody asks why** neither species leaves New Orleans. The absence is the seed.
3. Book 2's hook to Book 3: *one of the rotating names is on a wolf grave in Barataria* (`SERIES_BIBLE.md`).
4. **Hypnosis cannot manufacture desire.** Witches can't compel a vampire directly, raise the dead, break a blood bond, or out-fight anything. Ondine survives by preparing the room (`MAGIC_AND_POWER_RULES.md` §5, §7).
5. **Witch magic is contractual.** Every working costs the client something enforceable: a year, a name, a memory, an open-ended favor. Her ledger is her weapon (§7).
6. **Banned systems** across all narrators: arithmetic, math as metaphor, load-bearing, *file* as a cognitive verb, *pricing* people. Ondine's "That'll cost" lives only in her dialogue.
7. **Present tense, first person, American English.** No retrospective narrator.
8. Lucian's book-brand *ghost* belongs to Lucian and Selene. Book 2's brand is Silas's ***witch***, in dialogue only.
