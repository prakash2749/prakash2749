# PROLOGUE

## §1 — Cassian · 600

The council chamber, mid-battle. He moves through it toward his friend.

**Opening:** *The boy should see this. He never will, and he'll spend the rest of his life deciding what it was.*
Lucian named in the first three sentences — the hero is present by reference inside the first 500 words.

Bloodsteel, three sentences, no more. Aurelian's regeneration stops. He looks at Cassian and understands.

**Closes:** *"You kept it."*
**Cliff 10.** The reader now has the murderer and thirty-four chapters of watching Lucian thank him.

## §2 — Lucian · 600

Six days later. The largest vampire gathering in generations. Grief turned to rage, and Cassian at the front of the room performing devastation perfectly — because it isn't a performance.

Lucian walks in. The room changes.

**Closes:** short, ≤10 words. Something that is not a threat and reads as one.
**Cliff 9.**

## Drafting order

Not chapter order. Ch. 8, 28, 31, and 34 set the ceiling for everything around them, and the two interstitial rules — *show choosing, never explaining* — are easiest to break early and hardest to fix late.

1. **Prologue** — establishes all three voices and the dramatic irony.
2. **Ch. 8** — the collision. If this scene isn't right, nothing after it is.
3. **Ch. 34** — the climax. Write it before the middle so the middle aims at it.
4. **Ch. 28** — Rémy's confession. The hinge the whole back half turns on, and the scene that earns Book 3.
5. **Ch. 31** — the feral moment. Exactly once; getting it late risks making it a pattern.
6. **I-1 through I-5 as a set** — five short pieces written together, so the escalation is visible and none of them explains anything.
7. Everything else in order.
