The Blood Code

Five books. One city. A different couple in each, and one question under all of them.

King of Teeth — Lucian and Selene

Vow of Poison — Silas and Ondine

Son of Wolves — Rémy and Isabeau

Heir of Ash — Tobias and Severine

God of Blood — Delphine and Elias

Each book has its own happy ending. Read them in order. The city remembers what you skip.
