I carry her upstairs because she lets me.

She puts her arms around my neck and lifts her feet off the ground and lets me take her weight, and she has never let anyone take her weight. I know it the way I know the things her blood told me: the stubborn, bone-deep habit of holding herself up in every room she’s ever stood in.

She lets go of it on the stairs, and she also bites my ear on the landing, hard, and laughs when I nearly drop her.
