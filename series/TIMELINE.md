# Timeline — *The Blood Code*

Absolute years assume Book 1 opens in the present day (≈2026). **Relative distance is canon; absolute years are convenience.** If a drafted scene needs a year, take it from here rather than inventing one.

Rule: characters do not recite dates. Vampires think in eras and grudges. A four-hundred-year-old does not say "in 1986" — he says "when your father signed that thing with the wolves."

---

## Deep past — undated on purpose

**The Sundering.** One thing came apart and produced two halves. Vampires kept the thirst, the stillness, the long memory. Wolves kept the body, the speed, the pack. No document, no character, and no narrator dates this. Book 5 supplies the how.

Verity goes dormant beneath the ground that will eventually be New Orleans.

**Neither species has a creation story.** Both have customs whose origins they've lost. That absence is the setup — do not replace it with folklore.

## The city

**~1718.** New Orleans is founded by humans. Both species arrive inside a generation, and both have always explained it with commerce. Neither has ever examined the pull.

Because they arrived together, both species' oldest local institutions date to the same decade — the vampires' interment vault and the pack's ritual ground were established within a few years of each other. Book 3 notices they sit above the same point.

**~1718–1980s.** Roughly three centuries of contested territory. Cyclical wars, none decisive. Aurelian holds the city for the last three hundred of those years.

**Critical for Book 5:** these wars were never large enough. Skirmishes and feuds kept Verity dormant but never fed her. She needs an extinction event, not a fight — which is why Cassian's goal is not to *win* a war but to start a total one.

## The sixty years that made the series

**~1966 — Juliette's execution.** *Sixty years before Book 1.* Juliette, 78, kills a human woman for pleasure. She is caught, tried under the Code, and found guilty. She is not framed, not manipulated, and not sorry.

Cassian begs. Aurelian refuses, and performs the execution himself — drained, then beheaded, in front of witnesses.

Isabeau, ~154, says out loud that her sister deserved it. Her father never speaks to her the same way again.

**What Cassian will not look at:** he had been a quiet Code-skeptic for three centuries before Juliette was born and raised both daughters inside that contempt. Juliette killed because she was taught, patiently, at home, that the Code was a leash. Isabeau was taught the same thing and chose differently. Cassian needs Aurelian to be guiltier every year, because the alternative is a question about himself.

**Verity has been cultivating him for four hundred years** — since roughly 1626, three and a half centuries *before* Juliette died. The grief was not the beginning. It was the harvest.

**~1986 — the truce.** *Forty years before Book 1.* Aurelian and Gaspard Thibodeaux end the last open war. Four terms: boundaries, no warfare, council disputes, each leader answerable for their own.

Note the order: **the truce comes twenty years after Juliette.** Cassian was already grieving when he watched his friend make peace with animals. His politics and his wound have been the same thing for forty years, which is why nobody around him can tell them apart.

## Four years before Book 1

**~2022.** Daniel Ward, a court translator doing work for Aurelian, learns that Rémy Thibodeaux has been meeting a vampire from the King's inner circle. He says so to the wrong person.

Cassian tells Rémy that Daniel is a vampire informant whose report will get Gaspard assassinated. Rémy, 27, kills Daniel and Marisol.

Selene is 21. She inherits a bound ledger of her father's translation jobs, in which one recurring client is recorded only by a house-mark. She has no idea what it is, and she keeps it for four years because it was his.

Cassian believes the family is silenced. He is wrong about the daughter.

## Consistency checks

Run these against any draft that touches a date.

- Isabeau, 214, was ~154 at Juliette's execution sixty years ago. ✅
- Isabeau is Juliette's **elder** sister by ~76 years. Juliette is the one who died. Do not swap them.
- Cassian, 690, fathered Isabeau at ~476 and Juliette at ~552. Vampire conception is rare and difficult, which is why two children in 690 years reads as a lot and why each one mattered enormously.
- Lucian, 412, predates the city by a century. He is not a New Orleans native and occasionally sounds like it.
- Cassian, 690, is older than Lucian, 412, **and loses a straight fight.** Age is the floor, not the ceiling. Establish early and honestly.
- Juliette's execution (60 yrs) precedes the truce (40 yrs). Never reverse this — Cassian's grief must predate his politics.
- Verity's cultivation of Cassian (~400 yrs) precedes Juliette's birth (~138 yrs) by a wide margin. The grief was harvested, not planted.
- Selene was 21 when her parents died and is 25 now. Rémy was 27 then and is 31 now. Every character in every sexual situation in this series is an adult, on the page.
