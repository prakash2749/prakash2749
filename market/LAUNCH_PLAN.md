# Launch Plan — *The Blood Code*

Written 2026-09-25. LO's decision: **Book 1 does not launch until Book 2 (*Vow of Poison*) is drafted.** Book 1 launches with Book 2 on pre-order 60–90 days out, and Book 3 follows on the same spacing. This file is how to use that.

**Gating items as of 2026-09-27:** the manuscript is finished; there are no more text passes on Book 1. What now limits sales is the **cover** (not yet commissioned) and the **review count** at launch (the ARC team, §3). Both matter more than any further edit.

This is the legitimate route to the top of the charts. There's no guaranteed #1 and no guaranteed all-time bestseller, and anyone who promises one is selling something. What this plan does is stack every lever that actually moves rank on Amazon, in the order that makes them compound.

---

## 1. Why this order wins

Kent and Carlton don't top the charts with one book. They top them with a **reader who finishes one book and buys the next that night.** Amazon's algorithm rewards exactly that: sales velocity, Kindle Unlimited borrows (which count toward sales rank), read-through, and also-bought links between your own titles.

So the unit that sells isn't *King of Teeth*. It's *King of Teeth* plus a live pre-order for *Vow of Poison* on the last page. The teaser and the Thank You page are already built for it.

## 3. ARC team (honest reviews only)

- Recruit 50–100 readers through BookSirens, BookFunnel, or StoryOrigin ARC campaigns, and through dark-romance reader groups that allow ARC calls.
- Ask for an **honest** review on release day, with the standard disclosure line: *"I received a free advance copy and am leaving this review voluntarily."*
- No review swaps, no "friends and family" reviews, and no paying for reviews. Amazon removes them and can close the account (§8).
- Track who actually posts. The ones who do are the team for Book 2.

## 4. Paid promotion stack

Stack these across days 0–4 so the rank curve climbs instead of spiking once:

| Day | Promo type | Examples |

| 1 | Romance deal newsletter | Red Feather Romance, Fussy Librarian (romance list) |

Most of these want a minimum review count or rating, so book them only after the ARC reviews are live. **BookBub Featured Deal** is the biggest single lever in the genre and is highly selective: apply once the book has a solid review base. It's ideal for Book 1 as the series entry point when Book 2 or 3 launches.

## 5. Short-form video (BookTok and Reels): 30-day content calendar

The raw material is already in `market/POSITIONING.md`: the hooks, the twelve screenshot lines, and the trope stack. **Never post the killer's name, that the killer is his uncle, *Verity*, or that Selene doesn't take her revenge.**

| Week | Format | Content |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | "POV:" hooks | *POV: you're the bartender he fed on in a back hallway and it didn't work.* *POV: he erases every woman he feeds on. Then one asks if he's a vampire.* |
| 1 | Trope carousel | Vampire king · he can't erase her · touch her and die · revenge · morally black · blood pact · 412/25 |
| 2 | Screenshot lines | One line per post, dark background, no caption. Rotate through lines 1–12. |
| 2 | "Red flag / green flag" | *He broke a man's hand for pointing at her* (red) · *she says stop and he stops, even then* (green) |
| 3 | Chapter-one read-aloud | The opening of Ch. 1 over a Frenchmen Street bar ambience. It's the Look Inside, so readers can keep reading for free. |
| 3 | Villain angle | *The villain narrates five chapters. The hero thanks him in every one.* No names. |
| 4 | Countdown and launch | Cover reveal, release-day link, "it's live," and reader reactions (reshared with permission). |
| After launch | Book 2 bridge | *Every Silas and Ondine moment in Book 1, in order.* |

Post at a steady cadence. One consistent account that posts daily beats a burst of ten videos and then silence.

## 6. Amazon Ads

1. **Auto campaign**, $10–20 a day for 14 days, starting launch day. It finds the search terms and product pages that convert.
2. **Manual campaigns** built from what the auto campaign found:
   - **Keyword targeting:** the converting search terms and the seven KDP keywords.
   - **Product targeting:** comp titles' product pages (targeting competitor ASINs is allowed in ads, unlike in keywords).
3. Kill any target that spends 2–3× the book's price without a sale. Scale the ones that convert.
4. Once Book 2 exists, advertise **Book 1 only.** Read-through does the rest.

## 7. Brand handle and reader retention without a newsletter

The pen name is **Aurelia Thorn**, and it's set in the build. Use it identically on KDP, Author Central, Goodreads, TikTok and Instagram.

There's no newsletter, so the channels that bring a reader back for Book 2 are:
- **Amazon's Follow button.** When a reader follows the author page, Amazon emails them about new releases. The Thank You page asks readers to follow.
- **Book 2's pre-order,** live on release day and linked from the series page, so readers finishing Book 1 can buy it immediately.
- **Your social accounts,** as the one channel you fully control.
- **The bonus scene, *The Ghost That Stayed*.** With no newsletter, use it as ARC-team thank-you content, or as launch-week content posted in excerpts, or add it to the back of the book as a bonus chapter.

## 8. What would sink it (never)

- **Buying reviews, review swaps, or reviews from family and friends.** Amazon detects the connections. Reviews vanish and the account can be closed.
- **Click farms, bought KU page reads, or "borrow" services.** KDP has closed accounts and clawed back royalties for page-read manipulation.
- **Keyword stuffing, or other authors' names in the metadata or keywords.** It violates KDP's rules and gets the listing suppressed.
- **Mislabeling the category** (for example, putting explicit content in non-romance or children's categories) to chase an easy #1 flag.

## 9. Rank math: rough, honest ranges

These swing by season and year. Check them live with any BSR calculator the week before launch.

| Target | Roughly what it takes (ebook sales plus KU borrows per day) |
|---|---|
| #1 in a paranormal-romance subcategory (e.g. Vampires) | often ~50–300 a day |
| Top 100 in the whole Kindle Store | often ~1,000+ a day |
| #1 in the whole Kindle Store | often several thousand a day |

A debut with a strong ARC team, a stacked promo week, and a book that converts can realistically take a **category #1 flag**, which is the orange Best Seller badge. The overall top 100 is the job of the series, not of one book, and it's why Books 2 and 3 are on the calendar.

## 10. Watch these numbers, not your ego

- **Read-through**, Book 1 → Book 2 (target 50%+ in this genre).
- **KENP read per day** after the price rises.
- **Ad cost versus royalty**, per campaign.
- **Look Inside conversion.** If page views don't turn into sales, the problem is the cover or the blurb, not the book.
